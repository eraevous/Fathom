## The time_series knob (narrower readings, recorded)
1. **What is suggested.** Only what the report itself would ask about when nothing is declared
   (`timeseries.undeclared`): a temporal column with at least 30 distinct periods, as event counts or
   as the sum of a numeric column that is not identifier-like. Capped at 12 options. Each one is
   unticked and carries that observable reason. Other series stay YAML-only, and the page says so.
2. **`by` and granularities** are per-series controls: `by` from categorical or boolean columns, and
   granularity checkboxes for auto, hour, day, week and month. No granularity ticked means the loader
   default (`auto`), so nothing is emitted for it.
3. **`max_lag` and `top_n` are not offered.** They are tuning, not declarations. Values from
   `--config` are carried through unchanged and shown as "kept from --config".
4. **`value: null` is always written** for event-count series, so the YAML says what the series is.
5. The test `test_time_series_status_is_detected_not_assumed` keeps its name because FT-CFG-E1@3
   cites it (AEC code references). It now checks loader-key detection and the "not offered" wording.
